Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

What Events Desk does with your Google account

Events Desk asks for one Google permission: access to the specific Google Drive files it creates. It uses that permission to create a Google Sheet holding an outreach list in your Drive, update that Sheet, and read decisions typed into its Review tab.

Events Desk cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

What is stored

Events Desk stores a sign-in token so it can reach those files, the identifier and link of each Sheet it creates, and the decisions it reads from them. This data stays on the system of whoever runs Events Desk.

Sharing

A Sheet is shared only with the addresses the person running Events Desk sets as editors. Google user data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Removing access

You can remove Events Desk's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Sheets it created stay in your Drive until you delete them.
